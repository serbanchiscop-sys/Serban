Family Moments AI ("the App", "we", "us") is operated by **[YOUR LEGAL NAME /

## 1. Who this policy is for
The App is intended for use by **adults (18+)** who create and manage a family
account. Photos and information about children are uploaded **by the parent or
guardian who controls the account**. We do not knowingly allow children to
create their own accounts.

## 2. What we collect

| Data | Why we collect it |
|---|---|
| **Email address** | To create your account and sign you in (passwordless 6‑digit code). |
| **Photos & videos you upload** | To store them securely and show them in your private family timeline. |
| **Names & details you add** (family members, children, dates) | To organise your library and label milestones. |
| **AI prompts** (questions you type to the assistant) | To answer them. |
| **Purchase records** | To manage your subscription and print orders. |
| **Basic app/usage diagnostics** | To keep the App reliable. |

We do **not** sell your data. We do **not** show third‑party ads.

## 3. How your photos are processed by AI
When you use AI features (captions, search, stories, reels, books, milestone
detection), the relevant photo(s) and/or text are sent securely to our AI
provider, **Anthropic (Claude)**, solely to generate that result for you.

- Anthropic processes this data **only to return the result** and, per their
  API terms, **does not use it to train their models**.
- AI processing happens on our secure server; your AI provider key is never
  stored in the App.

## 4. Where your data is stored
Your photos, videos and account data are stored using **Supabase** (our
hosting/database/storage provider) on secure servers located in **[REGION,
e.g. the EU]**. Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS).

## 5. Third parties we share data with (processors)
We share the minimum necessary data with trusted providers who act on our
instructions:

- **Supabase** — secure storage, database, authentication.
- **Anthropic (Claude)** — AI features (see §3).
- **RevenueCat & Apple** — managing subscriptions.
- **Stripe** — processing payments for **physical** print/gift orders.
- **[PRINT FULFILMENT PARTNER]** — printing and shipping physical orders (name + shipping address only).

## 6. Your rights
Depending on your location (e.g. under **GDPR** in the EU/UK), you have the
right to access, correct, export, or **delete** your data, and to withdraw
consent. You can delete your account and all associated photos at any time from
**[Settings → Delete account]**, or by emailing us. We will respond within
**30 days**.

## 7. Data retention
We keep your data for as long as your account is active. When you delete your
account, we delete your photos, videos and personal data from active systems
within **[30/90] days** (backups roll off shortly after).

## 8. Children's privacy
The App is designed for parents to preserve memories of their own families. We
do not knowingly collect data directly from children under **[16]**. Because a
parent/guardian uploads content about their children, that parent is the data
controller for their family's content and consents to its processing on their
family's behalf.

## 9. Security
We use industry-standard measures (encryption in transit, access controls,
isolated per-family data). No system is perfectly secure, but we work hard to
protect your memories.
